Portfolio/studio images are separately extracted from three coordinated 1536×1024 concept sheets into 764×508 WebP photographs. The hero and headphone study retain their 1536×1024 source resolution. `-small.webp` derivatives provide smaller responsive versions; portfolio files use 480px widths, and the hero uses a 768px width. All derivatives keep the original aspect ratio.

Replace each image with an authorized photograph of your own product. Preserve its filename or update `src/data/site.ts`, the hero in `src/App.tsx`, and the relevant section's image path. Regenerate smaller derivatives when replacing images and keep alt descriptions accurate. Related sections may intentionally reuse the relevant project's cover; each of the ten portfolio projects has a different cover composition.

The comparison uses one matching concept image with a CSS tone treatment. It is labeled illustrative, not an authentic original/retouch pair. Replace both layers with a genuinely aligned pair before claiming an actual retouch demonstration. Motion entries are storyboard concepts, not playable videos.
